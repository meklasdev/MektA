# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MektA to be a welcoming, respectful and technically focused project. Contributors are expected to act professionally and to treat other people with dignity regardless of experience, identity, background or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Explain technical disagreement with evidence.
- Welcome beginner questions and help people learn.
- Keep feedback focused on the code, design or proposal.
- Respect privacy and never publish secrets or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled fairly. A report should include the relevant issue, pull request, message or other context.

This policy is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
